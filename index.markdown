## Our Aims

1. To promote equality and diversity in cycling.
2. The provision of a safe, collaborative space for cycling and maintenance activities.
3. To facilitate the sharing of knowledge, experience and skills related to cycling.
4. To accept the leadership of LGBTQ people and people otherwise marginalised within the cycling industry.
