## Welcome to our lending library!

We offer a range of equipment: panniers, saddles, tents, sleeping bags and more - borrow what you need when you need it.

[Browse our inventory here](https://dynamoglasgow.myturn.com/library/inventory/browse)!

The library is open for **collection & returns from 10:00 – 12:00 on Tuesdays and 17:00 – 19:00 on Thursdays**. 

## Memberships

Regardless of the membership option, you can borrow up to 5 items at a time, for up to 10 days. 

**One-off (£5 - £10 per item)**
- Valid for borrowing items once.
- If you think you would borrow more than once during the season, the annual membership is likely going to be cheaper!

**Annual (£50)**
- Valid for one full year from the first time you borrow any items.
- Enjoy **10% off all stock** in our shop. For example if you borrowed a saddle and loved it, you could then buy that saddle from us with this discount!

**Lifetime (£300)**
- This membership does not expire and gives you all the perks of an annual membership, plus you get to invest in supporting dynamo.
- Be part of a lasting contribution to support and grow our community!

## Borrowing any items

- [Browse our inventory online](https://dynamoglasgow.myturn.com/library/inventory/browse) or come into the shop to have a look around. If you're browsing online, you can reserve items online for the required duration. If you need an item for longer, we can agree this when you come to collect things in person.
- Choose the membership option that suits you & collect your items.
- Go have a great trip!
- Return your items. These should be returned clean and on the agreed date.

## How to get a membership:
- [Create a MyTurn account](https://dynamoglasgow.myturn.com/library/createUser/index) (you can do this yourself or we can create an account for you).
- Show us your photo ID* and proof of address in person at Dynamo.
- Pay for membership in person at Dynamo.

*don't worry if your ID doesn't look like you any more or you have changed your name

## Need advice?
- Send us an email at [hello@dynamocic.org](mailto:hello@dynamocic.org) with any questions or come into the shop.
- We can show you how to fit any bags etc. to your bike when you come to collect items.
