## Friends of Dynamo

Dynamo Glasgow CIC is a queer-led community bike workshop, run by our lead mechanic, seasonal staff, dedicated members and volunteers. 

**Our monthly expenses for our space are about £1,500** (rent, utilities, insurance). As a community bike shop, we want to keep our services affordable and accessible, while also creating space for community projects and activities. We aim to be as self-sustaining as possible through our workshop, but rising overheads can make this challenging. 

**Friends of Dynamo** supports our financial security, allowing us to cover our core costs while putting more time and resources into the projects that make cycling more accessible across Glasgow.  

Whether you choose to make a monthly contribution or a one-off donation, every bit of support helps us keep Dynamo going and continue building a more inclusive cycling community.
